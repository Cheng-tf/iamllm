About

Hi, I am Tengfei Cheng. I majored in Computer Science and Technology at Guangxi University. I study how intelligent agents can reason, act, and improve through interaction and feedback. I began my research in time series forecasting with Prof. Yunxuan Dong at Guangxi University. Then, I worked as a Research Assistant at the University of Liverpool (supervised by Prof. Meng Fang), and at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (supervised by Prof. Ying Wen and Prof. Yang Li), focusing on multimodal embodied agents. I plan to pursue a master's degree and continue research on LLM-based and RL-based agents.
