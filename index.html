About

Hi, I am Tengfei Cheng. I majored in Computer Science and Technology at Guangxi University (GPA: 3.42/4.0). I study how intelligent agents can reason, act, and improve through interaction and feedback. My work has led to a co-first-author paper, VaseVQA, at Findings of EACL 2026, and a first-author paper on multi-periodicity learning for tidal current forecasting in Electric Power Systems Research.

I began my research in time series forecasting with Prof. Yunxuan Dong at Guangxi University, then worked on multimodal agents with Prof. Meng Fang at the University of Liverpool. From June to September 2026, I was a Research Assistant at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Ying Wen and Prof. Yang Li. I plan to pursue a master's degree and continue research on embodied and self-improving agents, focusing on how evaluation and feedback can guide more reliable behavior.

Experiences

Jun. 2026 – Sep. 2026
Research Assistant @ Shanghai Jiao Tong University

With Prof. Ying Wen and Prof. Yang Li, I studied embodied-agent harnesses that combine VLM-based planning with reusable skills. I explored offline evolution of skills and skill cards from execution traces, aiming to improve skill selection and task completion while keeping the base models fixed.

Jun. 2025 – Present
Research Intern @ University of Liverpool

With Prof. Meng Fang, I study how evaluation can guide more reliable multimodal reasoning and decision-making. I developed benchmarks and an evaluation-guided post-training framework to examine spatial reasoning, refusal robustness, and compositional generalization. This work led to VaseVQA, a co-first-author paper at Findings of EACL 2026.

Mar. 2024 – Jun. 2025
Undergraduate Researcher @ Guangxi University

With Prof. Yunxuan Dong, I studied tidal current forecasting under interacting periodic patterns. I developed and evaluated a wavelet-enhanced convolutional framework to capture these patterns and their local variations. This work led to my first-author paper in Electric Power Systems Research.
